# Offset Assure

**For ISO/IEC 27001:2022.** Build the management system, prove the controls,
and walk into the certification audit ready. On your own server.

## Who it is for

Organisations working towards ISO 27001 certification, or keeping it: from a
first-time small company to an established ISMS team, and the consultants who
support them.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **The standard** | All 93 Annex A controls in 4 themes, with clauses 4 to 10 covered by the plan |
| **Statement of Applicability** | A decision and a reason on every control, a warning when an excluded control is still treating a risk, and the SoA as a PDF |
| **Guidance on every control** | What it means, what to do, what an auditor looks for, and what proof to keep |
| **Get ready** | A plan of 8 stages and 36 steps, from setting up to the certification audit. 23 steps tick themselves from your data |
| **Sample library** | 132 assets and 111 risks, covering every Annex A control, to add with one click |
| **Registers** | Evidence, risks, assets, policies, tasks, incidents and findings, plus suppliers, training, objectives, interested parties, and audits and reviews |
| **Corrective action** | Findings with root cause, action taken and verification, as clause 10.2 asks |
| **Control testing** | A dated record of each test and its result, to show a control operates over time |
| **Calendar and gap analysis** | Everything due in one list, and what is left or claimed without proof |
| **8 policy templates** | Word documents to start from, including the information security policy |
| **Reports (PDF, with your logo)** | Executive summary, Statement of Applicability, gap report, readiness plan, management review pack, document control list, risk register, evidence register |
| **Spreadsheets and example data** | CSV export and import on every register, and example data you can load and remove |
| **Help and Documentation** | 10 pages in the product, including an ISO 27001 certification playbook and where to find evidence in Microsoft 365, AWS and Google |

## Every Offset product also has

HTTPS with your certificate · four roles · every change recorded in an audit trail that auditors can read and download · nightly
backups and restore from the screen · signed one-click updates · email
reminders and a daily digest · Forgot password for administrators.

## How it installs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | One installer. Nothing else to install |
| **Linux** | A package that installs as a system service. Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Amazon Linux, SUSE |
| **Docker** | Pull the image and run it with Compose |
| **Needs** | A 64-bit server, about 300 MB of disk plus your evidence, and one port. It uses under 150 MB of memory at rest |
| **Sized for** | Up to about 50 users |

## Your data stays yours

It runs on your server, inside your network. No cloud, no telemetry, no
licence server. Offset Security has no access to it. Details are in the
security overview and the privacy sheet.
