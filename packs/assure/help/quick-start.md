# Quick start

Fifteen minutes. By the end you will know what the product holds, and what to
do tomorrow.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you a username, a password and the address. The first
person ever to open a new install creates the administrator account.

## Look at Get ready

Eight stages, 36 steps, from setting the product up to passing a certification
audit. Each step says what to do, why it matters, and has a button to the
screen where you do it.

Two kinds of step:

- **Checked for you.** The product looks at its own data and turns them green.
- **Ticked by you.** Things no software can see, such as whether management
  approved a policy.

## Fill in the two boxes that everything hangs off

**Applicability** holds your ISMS scope and your risk method. A paragraph each
is enough to start.

## Put something in three registers

- **Assets:** open the **Sample library** tab and add the ten that look like
  yours.
- **Risks:** the same. There are over a hundred to react to, each already
  pointing at the controls that treat it.
- **Policies:** open **Templates** and download the master information security
  policy. Put your name in it, have it approved, then record it.

Reacting to a list beats staring at a blank page, which is where most first
attempts stop.

## Decide on ten controls

**Applicability** lists all 93. For each: does it apply, and why. Ten today is
better than 93 next month.

## Attach one piece of proof

**Evidence → Add.** Attach a file, date it, and link it to a control. That is
the loop you will repeat for the rest of the programme: decide, do, prove.

## Tomorrow

Open **Calendar**. Anything with a date on it is there. If it is empty, nothing
is due — which is worth knowing too.
