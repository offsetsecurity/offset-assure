## 1. Purpose

This handbook sets the security rules for people at every stage of employment at <Company name>: before they join, while they work here, when their role changes, and when they leave.

## 2. Before employment

- Screening.Background and reference checks are done before access is given, in proportion to the role. Roles with privileged or financial access get more checks, as far as <the applicable law> allows.
- Terms of employment.Contracts say what the person must do to protect information, including duties that continue after they leave.
- Confidentiality.Everybody signs a confidentiality agreement before they get access to company information.

## 3. During employment

- Induction.Everybody completes security awareness training in their first <number> days, and again at least once a year. Completion is recorded.
- Competence.Managers make sure their people have the skills their security duties need. Gaps are closed with training, and the records are kept.
- Rules.Everybody follows the Acceptable Use Policy (POL-02) and reports suspected incidents straight away.

## 4. Disciplinary process

Breaking a security rule is dealt with through <Company name>'s disciplinary process, which is fair, written down and known to all staff. The outcome is in proportion to how serious the breach was and whether it was deliberate. It can range from retraining to dismissal and legal action.

## 5. Changing role or leaving

1. Tell IT.The manager tells <IT contact or mailbox> of the change or the leaving date at least <number> working days ahead.
2. Change of role.Access is reviewed and changed to fit the new role on the day it starts. Access from the old role is removed.
3. Leaving.All access is removed and all company property is returned (laptop, badge, tokens, files) by the end of the last working day.
4. Reminder.At the exit meeting the person is reminded that confidentiality continues after they leave.
5. Record.The leaver checklist is completed and kept as a record.

## 6. Contractors and temporary staff

The same rules apply to contractors and temporary staff. The contract or agreement with them says so. Their access ends on the contract end date unless it is formally extended.

## 7. Records

HR keeps: screening results, signed confidentiality agreements, training records, disciplinary records and leaver checklists, for <retention period> or as the law requires.
