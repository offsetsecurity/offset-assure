# ISMS document set (00 to 13)

> A starting point, not finished documents. Download them from **Policies** → **Templates**, put in your organisation's details, have them approved, then record each one in **Policies** with its owner and review date.

The set covers the documents ISO/IEC 27001:2022 expects you to keep. Each Word file has a page of instructions at the front. Delete that page when you are done.

## What is in the set

| No. | Document | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | ISMS master list of documents | The register of every controlled ISMS document, its owner, version and review date. |
| 01 | Scope of the ISMS | What the ISMS covers and what it leaves out, with the reasons (clause 4.3). |
| 02 | ISMS policy | The top-level information security policy and the commitments top management makes (clause 5.2). |
| 03 | Roles, responsibilities and authorities | Who is accountable for what in the ISMS (clause 5.3). |
| 04 | Risk assessment and risk treatment process | How you find, score and treat information security risks (clauses 6.1.2, 6.1.3, 8.2, 8.3). |
| 05 | Control of documented information | How documents and records are created, approved, changed, kept and retired (clause 7.5). |
| 06 | Information security communication plan | What is communicated, to whom, when and by whom (clause 7.4). |
| 07 | Procedure for internal audits | How the internal audit programme is planned, run and reported (clause 9.2). |
| 08 | Procedure for management review | How top management reviews the ISMS, and what the review must cover (clause 9.3). |
| 09 | Corrective action and continual improvement | How nonconformities are fixed and the ISMS is improved (clause 10). |
| 10 | Information security objectives plan | Measurable objectives, who owns them, and how progress is tracked (clause 6.2). |
| 11 | Statement of Applicability | An Excel workbook with one row for each of the 93 Annex A controls (clause 6.1.3 d). |
| 12 | Laws, regulations and contractual requirements | The legal, regulatory and contract rules that apply to you and how you meet them. |
| 13 | Risk treatment plan | The action, owner, deadline and residual risk for each risk you decide to treat. |

## Fill them in here

Press **Fill in** at the top of **Policies** → **Templates**.

1. **About your company** is asked once and used in every document.
2. **Your choices** already have the usual answer. Change any that do not fit.
3. Each document then asks only what is its own.

Your risks, objectives, suppliers, interested parties, issues, audits, findings, scope, policies and controls go in from the screens where you already keep them. You are not asked for them again.

Anything you leave empty stays yellow in the file. Download a document at any time, or **Download all filled (zip)** for the lot. It is made fresh each time, so it always matches your screens.

## Finishing in Word

1. Search for the symbol < to find any yellow blank left.
2. Check each section describes what you really do. Change it if it does not.
3. Get it approved, then use **Add to my policies** to record it.

## Good to know

- Document 11 is an Excel workbook. Fill in the yellow cells. The Summary sheet counts the results for you.
- Documents 00, 12 and 13 are wide tables, so they are set out landscape.
- Some records, such as the live risk register and the Statement of Applicability, are also kept inside this product. The documents explain the method. The product holds the data.
