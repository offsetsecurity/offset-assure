# Common questions

**Do I have to do the stages in order?**
No. Get ready suggests an order most organisations find easiest. Do anything you
can already do.

**A step will not tick.**
Steps with "The product checks this one itself" go green from your data. The
line underneath says what is missing, such as "91 of 92 that apply have an
owner". Steps only a person can confirm are ticked by hand.

**Something does not apply to us.**
Every step can be excluded with a reason, and so can every control. Excluded
things stop counting against you. Be strict: "we have no premises" is a reason,
"we have not got round to it" is not.

**Why does the Statement of Applicability want a reason on controls we include?**
Because clause 6.1.3 asks for the reasons for inclusion as well as exclusion. A
few words is enough: "treats risk 12", "required by contract", "data protection
law".

**What is the contradiction warning?**
A control you excluded that one of your risks points at. Either the control
applies after all, or the risk should not point at it. Auditors spot it in a
minute.

**How fresh does evidence have to be?**
The product flags anything older than 90 days. Set a collected date on
everything; the Evidence screen and the calendar do the rest.

**We are two people. Is this overkill?**
Use Get ready, the sample libraries and the templates, and ignore what does not
fit. A small organisation certifies on the same standard with far fewer
documents.

**Does this make us certified?**
No. It is where you record and show your work. Certification is between you and
an accredited certification body.

**Why does the browser say "Not secure"?**
The server has no certificate the browser trusts. An administrator fixes it
for everyone under **Settings → HTTPS certificate**, with a certificate from
your IT team. It is free: you do not need to buy one.
