## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the day-to-day rules for using <Company name>'s information, devices, networks and services. Everybody who uses them must read and accept it. The acceptance is recorded.

## 2. General use

- Business use.Company systems and information are for business. A little personal use is fine if it does not get in the way of work or create a risk.
- Passwords.Never share your password, a code or a security key with anybody, including IT.
- Approved tools only.Use only software and online services that <Company name> has approved. Do not put company information into unapproved tools, including public AI services.
- Classification.Handle information according to its label. Do not send Confidential or Restricted information to a personal email or storage account.
- Monitoring.<Company name> may check how its systems are used, as far as the law allows, to keep them secure.

## 3. Devices

- Company laptops and phones stay enrolled in <device management tool>, with disk encryption and a screen lock turned on.
- Do not turn off or change security software.
- Do not leave a device logged in and unattended in a public place. Report a lost or stolen device straight away to <contact or mailbox>.
- Personal devices may be used for work only if <the personal device rule, for example 'they are enrolled and meet the same settings'>.

## 4. Working away from the office

- Use <the company VPN or secure access tool> when working on sensitive information away from the office.
- Avoid sensitive work on open public Wi-Fi without it.
- Take care that nobody can read your screen or overhear a call about sensitive matters.

## 5. Email, internet and messaging

- Be careful with links and attachments you were not expecting. If in doubt, report it to <contact or mailbox> and do not click.
- Use company email and approved messaging tools for company business.
- Do not use company systems for anything illegal, offensive or that harms <Company name>.

## 6. Premises

- Wear your badge in the office. Do not let people you do not know follow you through a secure door.
- Lock your screen when you step away. Keep sensitive papers and storage devices out of sight.
- Visitors are signed in and escorted in areas that are not public.

## 7. Reporting

If you clicked a suspicious link, lost a device, sent information to the wrong person, or noticed anything unusual, report it straight away to <contact or mailbox>. Reporting quickly is never punished. Hiding an incident is a breach of this policy.

## 8. Breaking the rules

Breaking this policy may lead to action under <Company name>'s disciplinary process, and, where the law is broken, to legal action.

## 9. Acceptance

I have read and understood this policy and agree to follow it.
