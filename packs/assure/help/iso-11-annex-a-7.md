# Annex A.7: Physical controls

The 14 physical controls protect places and equipment: perimeters and entry, offices and secure areas, monitoring, protection from fire, flood and power loss, and equipment from purchase to disposal.

If you work entirely in the cloud with no office, many of these are still relevant: to home working, to laptops, and to your cloud provider's data centres, which you cover through supplier assurance rather than your own locks. Exclude a control only where there is genuinely nothing for it to apply to, and say why.

Part of [the audit-ready handbook](iso-01-certification.md). Previous: [Annex A.6: People controls](iso-10-annex-a-6.md). Next: [Annex A.8: Technological controls](iso-12-annex-a-8.md).

## How Annex A works

Annex A is a reference list. You do not have to implement every control. Your risk assessment decides which you need, and the Statement of Applicability records every one of the 93, applied or not, with the reason. See [Clause 6](iso-04-clause-6.md).

Once a control is in your Statement of Applicability, the auditor expects to see it working. For each control below:

- **Purpose** is what the control is for.
- **What to do** is the usual way to meet it.
- **What the auditor checks** is how it is typically tested.
- **Have ready** is the evidence to collect and attach.

The control numbers and titles are from ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The wording under each one is ours, not the standard's. For full implementation guidance, read the same control in ISO/IEC 27002:2022.

Controls marked *new in 2022* did not exist in the 2013 edition.

**In Assure.** Open the control in **Controls**. Set its applicability and reason in **Applicability**, attach proof in **Evidence**, and record tests in **Testing**. The same guidance is shown on each control.

## Sites and secure areas

### A.7.1 Physical security perimeters

**Purpose.** Define secure physical perimeters around sensitive areas.

**What to do.** Establish physical boundaries and barriers around areas holding sensitive systems/info.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor inspects perimeters and boundary controls.

**Have ready.** Site/floor plan showing secure areas; physical barrier evidence.

### A.7.2 Physical entry

**Purpose.** Control who physically enters secure areas.

**What to do.** Use badge/entry controls and visitor logs for secure areas.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor reviews entry records and access to secure areas.

**Have ready.** Access control system records; visitor logs.

### A.7.3 Securing offices, rooms and facilities

**Purpose.** Physically secure offices, rooms and facilities.

**What to do.** Lock and restrict access to sensitive rooms and facilities.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor inspects physical security of key rooms.

**Have ready.** Physical security measures; access restriction evidence.

### A.7.4 Physical security monitoring *(new in 2022)*

**Purpose.** Monitor premises for unauthorised physical access.

**What to do.** Use CCTV, alarms and monitoring for sensitive locations.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks monitoring is in place and reviewed.

**Have ready.** CCTV/alarm configuration; monitoring/review logs.

### A.7.5 Protecting against physical and environmental threats

**Purpose.** Protect against physical and environmental threats.

**What to do.** Put in place fire, flood and power protections and monitoring.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks environmental protections and tests.

**Have ready.** Fire suppression; environmental sensors; maintenance/test records.

### A.7.6 Working in secure areas

**Purpose.** Set rules for working inside secure areas.

**What to do.** Define and enforce secure-area working procedures.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks secure-area rules exist and are followed.

**Have ready.** Secure area procedures; sign-in/supervision records.

### A.7.7 Clear desk and clear screen

**Purpose.** Keep desks clear and screens locked when unattended.

**What to do.** Enforce a clear desk / clear screen policy with spot checks.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor may inspect the workplace and check policy.

**Have ready.** Clear desk/clear screen policy; spot-check results; screen-lock settings.

## Equipment, media and supporting utilities

### A.7.8 Equipment siting and protection

**Purpose.** Site and protect equipment against damage and prying eyes.

**What to do.** Position equipment to reduce risk of damage and unauthorised viewing.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor inspects equipment placement.

**Have ready.** Equipment siting standards; server room/screen positioning evidence.

### A.7.9 Security of assets off-premises

**Purpose.** Protect assets used off-site.

**What to do.** Secure laptops and devices taken outside the premises (encryption, rules).

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks off-site asset protection.

**Have ready.** Off-site asset policy; device encryption reports.

### A.7.10 Storage media

**Purpose.** Manage storage media securely through its whole life.

**What to do.** Control media handling and securely dispose of or wipe media.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks media handling and disposal.

**Have ready.** Media handling policy; secure disposal/wipe records.

### A.7.11 Supporting utilities

**Purpose.** Protect equipment from utility failures.

**What to do.** Provide UPS/generators and maintain them.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks supporting utilities and tests.

**Have ready.** UPS/generator inventory; maintenance and test records.

### A.7.12 Cabling security

**Purpose.** Protect cabling from interference and damage.

**What to do.** Follow cabling standards and protect power/network runs.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor inspects cabling protection.

**Have ready.** Cabling standards; protected cable run evidence.

### A.7.13 Equipment maintenance

**Purpose.** Maintain equipment to keep it available and secure.

**What to do.** Follow maintenance schedules and log the work.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks maintenance is performed and recorded.

**Have ready.** Maintenance schedule; maintenance logs.

### A.7.14 Secure disposal or re-use of equipment

**Purpose.** Wipe or destroy data before disposing of or reusing equipment.

**What to do.** Securely erase or destroy media before disposal or reuse.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks disposal/wipe evidence.

**Have ready.** Secure disposal certificates; wipe logs.
