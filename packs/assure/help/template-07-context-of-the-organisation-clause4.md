## 1. Purpose

This document records the setting in which <Company name> runs its information security management system. It answers three questions from ISO/IEC 27001:2022:

- Clause 4.1.What outside and inside issues affect our ability to protect information?
- Clause 4.2.Who has an interest in how we protect it, and what do they need from us?
- Clause 4.3.So what does the ISMS cover, and what does it leave out?

The three belong together: the issues and the interested parties decide what we must protect, and the scope is the line we draw as a result. They also feed the risk assessment (clause 6.1.1).

*Guidance: An issue is a fact about your situation, for example 'we rely on one cloud provider'. A risk is something that might happen because of it. Write the issues here and the risks in the risk register.*

## 5. Link to risk (clause 6.1.1)

Each issue and interested party need is checked against the risk register, to make sure the risks that come from it have been found and assessed. When an issue changes, the risk register is checked again.

## 6. Review

This document is reviewed at least once a year, at each management review, and after a significant change: a new service, site or system, a merger, a move into a new country, or a major change in the law.
