# Annex A.6: People controls

The 8 people controls follow a person through their time with you: screening before they join, the terms they sign, training while they work, what happens if they break the rules, and what still applies after they leave. Remote working and reporting security events are here too.

Auditors test these by sampling people: a recent joiner, a recent leaver, someone who changed role. Keep the records per person.

Part of [the audit-ready handbook](iso-01-certification.md). Previous: [Annex A.5: Organisational controls](iso-09-annex-a-5.md). Next: [Annex A.7: Physical controls](iso-11-annex-a-7.md).

## How Annex A works

Annex A is a reference list. You do not have to implement every control. Your risk assessment decides which you need, and the Statement of Applicability records every one of the 93, applied or not, with the reason. See [Clause 6](iso-04-clause-6.md).

Once a control is in your Statement of Applicability, the auditor expects to see it working. For each control below:

- **Purpose** is what the control is for.
- **What to do** is the usual way to meet it.
- **What the auditor checks** is how it is typically tested.
- **Have ready** is the evidence to collect and attach.

The control numbers and titles are from ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The wording under each one is ours, not the standard's. For full implementation guidance, read the same control in ISO/IEC 27002:2022.

Controls marked *new in 2022* did not exist in the 2013 edition.

**In Assure.** Open the control in **Controls**. Set its applicability and reason in **Applicability**, attach proof in **Evidence**, and record tests in **Testing**. The same guidance is shown on each control.

## Before, during and after employment

### A.6.1 Screening

**Purpose.** Check people's backgrounds before hiring, proportionate to risk.

**What to do.** Run screening/reference checks appropriate to each role before granting access.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor samples new hires for screening evidence.

**Have ready.** Screening/background check records; reference checks.

### A.6.2 Terms and conditions of employment

**Purpose.** Put security responsibilities into employment terms.

**What to do.** Include security obligations in employment contracts.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor samples contracts for security terms.

**Have ready.** Employment contract security clauses; signed agreements.

### A.6.3 Information security awareness, education and training

**Purpose.** Keep everyone aware and trained in security, relevant to their role.

**What to do.** Deliver regular awareness training and record who completed it.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks training happened and coverage is high.

**Have ready.** Training records (Training); completion rates; materials.

### A.6.4 Disciplinary process

**Purpose.** Have a fair, formal process for security rule breaches.

**What to do.** Define a disciplinary process for security violations and make staff aware.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks a documented disciplinary process exists.

**Have ready.** Disciplinary policy/process; staff awareness evidence.

### A.6.5 Responsibilities after termination or change of employment

**Purpose.** Enforce security duties that continue after employment ends or changes.

**What to do.** Define surviving obligations and complete offboarding for every leaver/mover.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor samples leavers to confirm duties and access were handled.

**Have ready.** Post-employment clauses; offboarding records, attached as evidence.

### A.6.6 Confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements

**Purpose.** Protect shared information with confidentiality agreements.

**What to do.** Use NDAs with staff and third parties and keep them on file.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor samples for signed NDAs where required.

**Have ready.** Signed NDAs / confidentiality agreements.

### A.6.7 Remote working

**Purpose.** Secure remote and home working.

**What to do.** Set a remote working policy and enforce VPN and device security.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks remote-work controls are defined and applied.

**Have ready.** Remote working policy; VPN and device security settings.

### A.6.8 Information security event reporting

**Purpose.** Give staff an easy way to report security events.

**What to do.** Provide a reporting channel, make staff aware of it, and act on reports.

**What the auditor checks.** The auditor checks the channel exists, is known, and is used.

**Have ready.** Reporting channel/procedure; awareness evidence; reports received.
