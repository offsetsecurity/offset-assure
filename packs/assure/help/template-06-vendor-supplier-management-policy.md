## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how <Company name> chooses, contracts with and monitors suppliers and service providers, including cloud services, so that a supplier does not become the weakest point in our security.

## 2. Before using a supplier

1. Ask.Before signing, anyone who will have access to our information or systems is assessed: a short security questionnaire, any certificate they hold (for example ISO/IEC 27001 or SOC 2), and how they handle data.
2. Rate.Each supplier gets a risk level, High, Medium or Low, with the reason, recorded in the supplier register.
3. Contract.The contract includes the security terms in section 3.
4. Approve.<role title> approves the supplier before work starts.

## 3. What the contract says

- Confidentiality of our information.
- Telling us about a breach within <time, for example 24 hours>.
- Data protection terms, and where our data is stored and processed.
- Our right to ask for evidence, or to audit, where the risk justifies it.
- Returning or securely deleting our data when the contract ends, and confirming it in writing.
- Rules for the supplier's own suppliers who handle our information.

## 4. Cloud services

- Cloud services are chosen against clear security needs and set up to <the provider's hardening guide or our own standard>.
- Before depending on a critical cloud service, an exit plan is written: how to export our data, in what format, and how we confirm deletion.
- Who in <Company name> owns each cloud service is recorded in the supplier register.

## 5. While the supplier is working for us

| Risk level | Review |
|---|---|
| High | At least once a year: questionnaire, certificates, incidents |
| Medium | At least every <two years> |
| Low | When the contract renews |

- A change to the supplier's service, ownership or the companies it uses triggers a new assessment.
- A supplier incident that affects our information is handled under the Incident Response Plan (POL-05).

## 6. Suppliers of suppliers

For critical services, the assessment also looks at the supplier's own key suppliers, and at how much we depend on one provider. Where a risk cannot be dealt with in the contract, it is written in the risk register and either treated or accepted by <role title>.

## 7. Ending the relationship

- Access is removed on the last day.
- Our information is returned or deleted, and the supplier confirms it.
- The supplier register is updated.
