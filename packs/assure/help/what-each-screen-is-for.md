# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Golden thread** | Every risk, what treats it, and the proof, joined up. Green means satisfied, red means broken. A Clauses column shows whether 6.1.2, 6.1.3 and 8.3 are met for each risk, and what to do if not |
| **Dashboard** | Where the programme stands today, in numbers |
| **ISMS** | Clauses 4 to 6 on one page: context issues, interested parties, scope, risk method, objectives and your certificate |
| **Applicability** | The decision on all 93 Annex A controls. Scope and risk method show here too, the same text as on ISMS |
| **Controls** | Every control: status, owner, evidence, tests, notes |
| **Evidence** | The proof, with dates and what each item supports |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong, scored, owned and treated |
| **Assets** | What you hold that matters, and who owns it |
| **Policies** | Your documents, their versions and review dates. The Templates tab has eight to start from, and **Add to my policies** puts one in as a draft |
| **Tasks** | The work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Incidents** | What went wrong, how bad, and where it stands |
| **Findings** | Nonconformities and observations, with root cause and the fix |
| **Reports** | PDFs to hand to management or an auditor |
| **Suppliers** | Who you rely on, what they hold, and what assurance they gave. Has a sample library |
| **Training** | Who was trained, on what, and when it is due again |
| **Objectives** | What you are aiming at, how it is measured, and whether you got there |
| **Interested parties** | Who cares about your security, what they need, how you meet it |
| **Audits & reviews** | Internal audits and management reviews, planned and held |
| **Communications** | What you tell people about security, to whom, how often, and when it is next due |
| **Escalation** | Automatic reminders for anything with a date and an owner's email, one email each, and who is told as it goes overdue |
| **Calendar** | Everything with a date on it, in one list |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and what they are allowed to do |
| **Audit trail** | Who did what, when, and from where. Read only. Administrators and auditors |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, the daily digest, updates, and the HTTPS certificate |

## The two that surprise people

**Applicability is a document, not a screen.** What you fill in there is what
the auditor asks for first, in the form they expect it.

**The calendar is derived.** It stores nothing of its own. Every row belongs to
another screen, which is where you change its date.
