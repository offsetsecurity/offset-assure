## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out, in one place, how <Company name> protects the information it holds: its own, its customers' and its suppliers'. It turns the commitments in the ISMS policy (ISMS-02) into rules that people can follow day to day.

*Guidance: The ISMS policy says what top management has committed to. This policy says what everybody does about it. Keep the two short and separate.*

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to everybody who works for or on behalf of <Company name>, including employees, contractors and temporary staff, and to all information and systems inside the scope of the ISMS (see the Scope of the ISMS, ISMS-01).

## 3. Principles

- Protect information in proportion to its value and risk.Not everything needs the same protection.
- Give people only the access they need.Access is granted for a job, reviewed regularly and removed when it is no longer needed.
- Know what you hold.Every important asset has a named owner.
- Report problems early.Nobody is punished for reporting a mistake or a suspected incident in good faith.
- Meet the law and our contracts.Legal and customer requirements come first and are recorded in ISMS-12.

## 5. Responsibilities

| Who | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| Top management | Approving this policy, providing the people, time and money to follow it, and reviewing how it is working. |
| <ISMS manager role> | Keeping this policy current, running the ISMS day to day, and reporting to top management. |
| Managers | Making sure their teams know and follow the rules, and that access matches the job. |
| Everybody | Following the rules, completing training, and reporting anything that looks wrong. |

## 6. Breaking the rules, and exceptions

- Breaking this policy may lead to action under <Company name>'s disciplinary process.
- An exception must be asked for in writing, judged for risk by <role title>, approved for a fixed period and recorded.
- Suspected breaches go to <contact or mailbox>.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year, and after any serious incident or major change. The owner records each review in the version history.
